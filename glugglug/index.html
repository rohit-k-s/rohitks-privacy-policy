Privacy policy

Last updated 17 October 2026

In short

GlugGlug keeps your budgets and spending on your iPhone. We don’t collect, see or share any of it. There’s no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

What the app stores

The budgets, expenses, notes and settings you enter, in the app’s private storage on your iPhone. The app reads your iPhone’s time zone to work out dates. It doesn’t ask for your location, contacts, photos or any other permission.

Who can see it

Only you. The app doesn’t connect to the internet, so nothing you enter is sent to us or anyone else.

Backups

Like other apps’ data, your GlugGlug data can be included in your iPhone’s iCloud or computer backups, which Apple’s terms cover. Restoring a backup restores it.

Deleting your data

Use Settings › Delete all data in the app, or delete the app. Either removes everything it stored on your iPhone.

Apple

Apple handles downloads and updates, and may collect information under its own privacy policy. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone’s settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and usage totals. They don’t include anything you enter in GlugGlug.

Children

GlugGlug isn’t aimed at children, and it collects nothing from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update it here and in the app, with a new date.

Contact
